## Failures (all fixed this pass)

### Q1. Cost line quantity and unit rate clipped to wrong-looking numbers  [HIGH]
Environment: mobile 375, EN (mirror symptom in AR: cost item labels clipped to their tails)
Steps: 1. New proposal → add program 2. Enter qty 120, rate 185000 3. Look at the row.
Expected: the entered values readable.
Actual: fixed columns (w-24/w-32/w-32/w-10 = 392px) squeezed the inputs; "120" displayed as "12", "185,000" as "185". A reader takes wrong numbers off the screen (data credibility, HNI priority 6).
Fix applied: CostTable and the BOQ CostingWorkspace tables wrapped in `overflow-x-auto` with `min-w-[30rem]`/`min-w-[32rem]` and narrower numeric columns on mobile (`w-16/w-24/w-28` → `sm:` originals). Values never clip; the tail columns scroll inside the card.
Re-verified: qty shows "120", rate "185,000", `scrollWidth == clientWidth` on both inputs, EN and AR.

### Q2. Documents row actions unreachable  [HIGH]
Environment: mobile 375, both directions
Steps: 1. Mark a proposal sent 2. Open Documents.
Actual: the actions cell (Open document, View costing, Download Excel, Delete) was clipped away entirely by the `overflow-hidden` container; no way to open or delete a document on a phone.
Fix applied: inner `overflow-x-auto` wrapper + `min-w-[46rem]` table inside the rounded card.
Re-verified: scrolling the row reveals all four actions; open-document works.

### Q3. Client view toolbar: Save PDF cut off at the viewport edge  [MEDIUM]
Environment: mobile 375, both directions (clipped at the end side in each)
Actual: the trailing button group was `flex` without wrap; the primary Save PDF button rendered half off-screen.
Fix applied: `flex-wrap` on the group.
Re-verified: Save PDF fully visible on its own row, EN and AR.

### Q4. Client document unusable to read on a phone  [MEDIUM]
Environment: mobile 375, both directions
Actual: pages are a fixed 13.33in (1280px); a phone showed ~30% of a page and required blind panning.
Fix applied: scale-to-fit preview — ClientView sets CSS `zoom = containerWidth / 1280` (capped at 1) on `#client-document`, recomputed on resize; `print.css` resets `zoom: 1 !important` under `@media print` so PDF output is untouched. Browsers without `zoom` keep the old horizontal scroll.
Re-verified: full pages visible and readable at 375px in EN and AR; desktop unaffected (zoom 1).
